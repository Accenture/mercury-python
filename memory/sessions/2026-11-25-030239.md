## What was done

- **ask-person** (`extensions/ask-person`): `resources/rest.yaml` with four entries; the aiohttp handlers became
  private functions reading the engines' `AsyncHttpRequest` map - `ask.page` (the HTML shell or a notice with its
  status), `ask.wait`, `ask.reply`, `ask.config`; who is asking is resolved by two authentication functions,
  `ask.identity` (a person, by the configured mode) and `ask.reply.identity` (a person, or a channel app under its
  bearer naming the person in `decided_by`), whose verdict headers the edge leaves on the request as its session map
  (`who`, `via`). `rest.automation: true` in `application.yml`; the mount is gone.
- **agent-question** (`extensions/agent-question`): `resources/rest.yaml` with six entries behind `question.config`,
  `question.list`, `question.events`, `question.decide`, `transcript.list`, `transcript.show` and the authentication
  function `question.identity`. The device stream is a `stream: true` entry: an interceptor that reads
  `Last-Event-ID`, opens the stream at once (the head alone - a bare segment - with a day's idle allowance through
  the head's `x-ttl`) and follows the feed from a task of its own, so a device never holds a worker; the response
  header transform adds `X-Accel-Buffering: no`; the shutdown hook still ends every stream.
- **Host:** a keep-alive comment that cannot be written now ends the stream and closes its sink (`rest_automation._next`
  no longer swallows the failure - a device that left was otherwise held until the idle allowance, a day here);
  `EventBus.has_sink` and `EventStreamWriter.connected`, the pack's counterpart of a Java producer's
  `po.exists(replyTo)`; `Platform.add_routes` documented as the escape hatch for what an entry cannot express.
- **Tests:** the two extension suites serve the REST-enabled host from the default registry with the extension's own
  `rest.yaml`; the route-order tests became table assertions (the entries, their authentication, the private
  functions, nothing mounted); the stream test reads the engines' frames; a REST test pins that a client who leaves a
  stream is noticed at the next keep-alive (the sink closed, the producer's `connected` false). The shared vectors
  are unchanged.
- **Docs:** both extension READMEs, the root README's precedence paragraph, CHANGELOG Unreleased Changed 10.
