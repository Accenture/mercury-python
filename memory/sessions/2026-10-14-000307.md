## Decisions

- The Mantle client, not the legacy `AnthropicBedrock` (the InvokeModel path): the SDK's guidance names it the client for new code,
  and it exposes the same messages surface, so the helper's call plan is untouched.
- One optional key, `llm.aws.region`; unset, the SDK's own resolution (`AWS_REGION`, `AWS_DEFAULT_REGION`, a base URL override)
  applies. No profile key: `AWS_PROFILE` is the chain's own.
- The SDK's client-side refusal fallback middleware is not wired in; a refusal on Bedrock is the contract's 422 or `stop_details`.
  A follow-up if the drive shows refusals matter there.
