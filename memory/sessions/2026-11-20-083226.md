## What We Did

- **The recorder** (`extensions/ask-person/ask_person.py`): `question.audit`, a private function of the ask-person
  process registered at boot with `ask.audit.instances` workers, behind local requests - `person.ask` records the
  message before it delivers to the channels (so a device finds the row it is told of) and `POST /ask/reply` records
  the answer with the stamped decisions, who decided, when and the engine's outcome; a recorder that fails or is not
  there is logged by type and never fails a hold or a reply. The writes are order-independent: a wait upserts on its
  key unless an older message lands late, an advice appends (no state), the answer applies while the row is open or
  when the engine accepted it. The published contract `QuestionStore` with `MemoryQuestionStore` and
  `PostgresQuestionStore` (psycopg over the audit store's connection keys under `ask.audit.postgres.*`; the `question`
  table and `question_seq`, `schema/postgres.sql` version 2, `purge.sql`, the `dev/` scripts), a sweeper
  (`ask.audit.sweep`), health reporting the store and its counts.
- **Identity `none` or `oauth2`, `proxy` removed (R12).** The reply page is a shell: its script reads `GET /ask/config`,
  signs the person in by Authorization Code with PKCE when the installation asks (a public client in the browser, the
  token kept for the tab), reads the wait from the new JSON endpoint `GET /ask/{ticket}/wait` (the re-advice, never a
  resume, under the same identity as the reply) and posts the decision with the bearer; the server-side rendering of
  the form moved into the script. `ask.reply.auth.oauth2.client-id` and `.scopes` are the page's.
- **The shared vectors** are version 2: `record_cases`, `answer_cases` and `transition_cases` moved from the inbox's
  file (the `seen` step dropped, the answers keyed by the wait, a late older message and an answer with no recorded
  wait added), generated from the reference store. 121 tests; ruff clean, basedpyright 0, the whole suite 784 passed /
  76 skipped; a smoke run through `mercury-serve` (the recorder registered, the config endpoint, health with the
  record, the page's 404 for an unconfigured graph). Not run: the live PostgreSQL test (`ASK_AUDIT_TEST_URL`) - no
  database was up in this session.
- **A testing lesson:** the default registry's bus binds its workers to the event loop that first delivers, and
  pytest-asyncio gives every test its own loop - a fixture that sends over the shared bus must close it afterwards, or
  the next test's request waits on workers of a dead loop (a 408 that passed in isolation). The `host` and `recorder`
  fixtures close the bus as the `channel` fixture did; `shutdown()` drops the closed PostOffice so the next send
  builds a fresh one.
- The agent-question app is untouched here and keeps its inbox until the next step makes it a reader of the record.
